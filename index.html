    02 about

    Valentina Mount cuts photographs apart and pastes them back, builds a heart out of acrylic, paints and draws, models, photographs and makes signs and video.

    The collages are photo edits: halftone, duotone and grain, cut apart and pasted back together. The heart is cut from translucent burgundy acrylic, layered with silver webs and three spiders, and hung from a beaded chain. Family chain is a mixed-media painting full of charms; the lighter is a hand-drawn digital illustration. The rest is photographs and client work: a car meet, a graduation, modeling for a studio portfolio and for Zumiez, two jobs for Sign Shop LLC, and a Siesta G reel.

      collage

      five photo edits, each built around one figure. the same moves come back in new mixes: halftone dots, duotone colour, one half flipped to a negative, a cut-out pasted back in at pocket size. two run loud (pink with chrome spikes, magenta over cyan), two keep to black, white and a little red, and one is electric blue with pink in the skin.

      05 on paper

      on paper

      family chain mixes paint with things fixed to the surface: orchids, a leafy vine, thread and a strip of newspaper, so parts of it cast real shadows. the lighter collage goes the other way: drawn by hand, digitally, all flat colour and clean outlines.
